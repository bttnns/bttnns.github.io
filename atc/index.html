Support

Approach Control: ATC Sim

Something broken, a question, or an idea? Email atc@t5s.io. I read everything and reply as soon as I can.

The quickest way is Send Feedback in the app (Settings, or How to Play). It opens a message in your Mail app with your app version, device model and iOS version filled in, and you can read and change all of it before you send.

PRO and purchases

  • What is free? San Diego on the Graveyard shift. PRO unlocks everything else.
  • Restore a purchase. Open Settings, tap PRO, then Restore at the top of the page. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.
  • Manage or cancel. Once subscribed, Manage Subscription is on the PRO page and in Settings. You can also do it in your Apple Account settings on your device. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to avoid the next charge.
  • Refunds. Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Family Sharing. PRO can be shared with your family through Apple Family Sharing.

Your data

The game has no account and no server. Your settings, logbook and saved shifts live on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.
