How it plays

Pick a plane. Give the order.

Every plane you land earns points. Every mistake costs them. A shift ends after 30 landings or 45 minutes.

Turn

HDG 228

Drag from a plane to point it where you want it to go.

Descend

8,000

Drag ALT to bring it down. Drag SPD to slow it.

Land

ILS 27

Line it up with the dashed line. Tap ILS, and it lands itself.

Keep apart

3 NM

Three miles apart, or 1,000 feet above or below. Too close costs you.

Built for touch

Everything on one card.

Tap a plane and its card opens. Altitude, speed and heading are big and easy to change. Hold, Direct and Hand Off are one tap away.

Departures climb out through your airspace too. Get them to the edge, and keep them clear of the arrivals.

Why PRO

Made by one person who loves general aviation. I spent years as a Kubernetes engineer at a big tech company you likely know. Then came a reorg, my pet got cancer and died, and a manager told me I would never be a software developer. I was let go. So I built this. Turns out he was wrong.

I have always wanted to learn to fly, but lessons cost a fortune. PRO goes into my flying fund, and into my mortgage, because once the singularity shows up I am going to need it.

Questions

Good to know.

How do I join the beta?

Install TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad. Then open this link and tap Accept.

Is it really free?

Yes. San Diego on the Graveyard shift is the full game, not a demo. Play as long as you like.

How much is PRO?

Try it free for a week. Then it’s $4.99 a year. You can share it with your family.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. On iPad the scope fills the screen. With PRO, there’s an Apple Watch app too.

Do I need the internet?

No. The whole game works offline.

Is this real air traffic control training?

No. It’s a game, made with care. Don’t use it to fly.
