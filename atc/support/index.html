How do I join the beta?

Install TestFlight from the App Store. Then open this link and tap Accept.

What is free?

San Diego, the full shift. PRO unlocks everything else.

How much is PRO?

$7.99 a year. For launch, your first month is free. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, and you won’t be charged.

How do I restore or cancel PRO?

Restore: open Settings, tap PRO, then tap Restore. Cancel: go to your Apple Account settings, then Subscriptions.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There’s no account and no server. Read the privacy policy.
