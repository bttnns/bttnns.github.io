Playing

How do I land a plane?

Turn it toward the dashed line that leads to the runway, and bring it down. When the ILS button lights up, tap it. The plane flies itself down to the runway.

Why is the ILS button dark?

The plane isn’t ready yet. It has to be heading for the line gently, and low enough. The note under the button tells you what’s missing.

What counts as too close?

Two planes less than 3 miles apart, and less than 1,000 feet above or below each other. It costs points and part of your margin. If your margin runs out, the shift ends early.

How long is a shift?

A shift ends after 30 landings or 45 minutes, whichever comes first.

What is X?

X hurries one plane along. It turns, climbs and descends faster, but its landing earns no points. X comes with PRO, and you can set how strong it is in Settings.

PRO and purchases

What is free?

San Diego on the Graveyard shift, the full shift, not a demo. The tutorial, How to Play and the glossary are free too. PRO unlocks everything else.

See what PRO adds.

How much is PRO?

Try it free for a week, then it’s $4.99 a year. The free week is for new subscribers. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, and you won’t be charged.

How do I restore PRO?

Open Settings, tap PRO, then tap Restore at the top. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

Tap Manage Subscription on the PRO page or in Settings. You can also cancel in your Apple Account settings on your device. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid the next charge.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles all refunds. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can my family use PRO?

Yes. PRO works with Apple Family Sharing.

I bought PRO in an early version.

It stays yours. You don’t need to subscribe.

Apple Watch

The watch says PRO is locked.

The watch app unlocks when your iPhone has PRO. Open the game on your iPhone so it can tell your watch.

What does the watch show?

Your shift: the airport, its traffic, your points, margin, landings and violations. It taps you when planes get close or a pilot calls an emergency. Between shifts, you can play a small game with the Digital Crown.

Your data

Do my settings sync between devices?

Yes, if you use iCloud. Your game settings sync through your own iCloud account. Your logbook and saved shifts stay on each device.

How do I move my logbook to a new device?

It comes with your device backup when you restore. You can also export it as a file from the Approach Log, and import it on the other device.

Does the game collect my data?

No. There is no account and no server. Everything stays on your device. Read the full privacy policy.
