Privacy Policy

Approach Control: ATC Sim · Effective 23 Sep 2026 · Updated 10 Oct 2026

Approach Control is a single-player air-traffic control game. It does not create an account for you and it does not sell personal data. It has no servers of its own: nothing it stores is sent to us.

What the app stores on your device

  • Game settings (appearance, theme, haptics, and similar preferences).
  • Optional session resume files and a local shift logbook / stats history.
  • An offline hint of PRO unlock status. The real purchase record lives with Apple.
  • On TestFlight and test builds only: a flag that you claimed PRO as a tester. It stays on the device and is never synced.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, a fixed list of game settings (appearance, theme, sound, the clock and similar preferences) is kept in step between your devices through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, in your own iCloud account. Your purchase status, the tester flag, saved sessions and your logbook are never synced through iCloud.

Your logbook and statistics are included in your device’s own backup (iCloud Backup or a backup to your computer, as you have set it up), so they come with you to a new device when you restore it. You can also export them as a file from Statistics and import that file on another device. The app sends the file nowhere; you choose where it goes.

Apple Watch

If an Apple Watch is paired with your iPhone and has the watch app installed, the iPhone sends it what it needs to show the game. Apple’s WatchConnectivity carries it directly between your own devices; it does not pass through any server of ours. It is game state only, nothing about you.

  • With PRO: the scope’s theme and your appearance setting, that PRO is unlocked, and while a shift is open, its numbers: the airport, the points and the margin, landings, departures, violations, go-arounds, the planes on the scope, the clock, whether it is running, stopped or over, whether it is overtime or an open shift, and how it ended. These are rewritten when the shift changes and otherwise at most every 30 seconds. While the watch app is open, the iPhone also sends the live scope about once a second: the airport’s runways, the traffic on it (callsign, position, heading, altitude, and whether it is a departure) and its alerts. The iPhone also sends today: the date, the kind of day or the special day’s name, today’s worked, clean and busy marks, and your streak in days. Today is rewritten when it changes.
  • Without PRO: your appearance setting, and that PRO is locked. Nothing about a shift, no traffic, and nothing about today.

The watch keeps the last settings and shift numbers it received, on the watch, so it can show them when it opens. It does not keep the traffic. It keeps today the same way, until midnight. The game you play on the watch itself keeps its own tally on the watch. Once you have played it, the watch sends that tally to the iPhone, the same way, directly between your own devices: your best run, the triangles you have flown, the time you have flown, and the violations on its count. That is the only thing the watch sends. The iPhone keeps it on the device for the Approach Log; it is never synced through iCloud and never included in a file you export. Without a paired watch that has the app, nothing is sent.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends Apple the score of a finished shift for its leaderboard, and any achievements that shift earned. They are stored by Apple under your Game Center account, and Apple’s privacy policy covers them. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

Purchases

PRO is an auto-renewable subscription (and, for early players, a one-time purchase), processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app checks it on your device, from the signed record Apple keeps there; it is never sent to us or to any server of ours. We do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple’s privacy policy covers the transaction, and you manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Send Feedback

Send Feedback (in Settings and in How to Play) opens a new message in your own Mail app, addressed to us, with the app’s version, your device model, its iOS version and whether you have PRO written under your words. While Attach Last Shift’s Log is on, your last shift’s record from the logbook (its airport, shift, counts and the flights in it) is attached as a file. You can read and change all of it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you tap Send. We use what you send only to answer you and to improve the game.

Apple Intelligence

On a device with Apple Intelligence, while Write With Apple Intelligence is on in the app’s Settings, the app can ask Apple’s on-device model to write some of its words: pilots’ radio calls, a sentence about a landmark for the Supervisor’s handover at the start of a shift, one sentence of Today’s Briefing about who is flying on a special day, and a flight’s story. The model runs on your device, never on a server, and is given only game facts; the landmark sentence is written from a real fact in the airport’s own file, and the briefing sentence from the special day’s own line and the kinds of planes flying that day. The lines it writes are kept in the app’s own folder on the device. The handover may also mention your own record, such as your best points at that airport; the app works that out on your device from your logbook, and the model never sees it. If you type a question into the search of How to Play or the Logbook and send it, the app may give that question to the same on-device model to work out what you are asking; this is a feature still being tested and is off unless switched on, and the question never leaves your device.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising ID for ads.
  • No third-party analytics SDK.
  • No account email or password inside the app.

Crash and diagnostics

iOS gives the app daily performance and diagnostic reports (MetricKit). The app writes them to the device’s own log and sends them nowhere.

If you opt in through iOS, Apple may share anonymized crash diagnostics with the developer via App Store Connect. That is controlled in your device’s Analytics & Improvements settings.

Children

The app is a general-audience game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
